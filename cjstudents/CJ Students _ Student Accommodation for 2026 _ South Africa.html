Starting your student journey is an exciting step, and with CJ Students, it will be unforgettable. Our NSFAS-accredited student accommodation goes beyond just providing a place to stay. Our mission is to create a vibrant, supportive community where you can be social, have peace of mind, and thrive academically.

We focus on YOU.

Safe, secure and affordable housing is a given. To focus on your studies, you need to feel safe, so our state-of-the-art security, including 24/7 online monitoring and biometric access, ensures a safe environment where you can focus on what really matters: making memories, smashing goals, and embracing the student experience to the fullest.

We focus on COMMUNITY.

However, it’s our thriving student community that makes all the difference. Whether it’s connecting through study groups, joining in on lively social events, or just sharing a laugh in the common areas, we create spaces where confidence grows and you make friends for life. With academic workshops, mentorship programmes, and convenient campus transport, we’ll help you succeed inside and outside the classroom.

At CJ Students, you’re not just moving into a room – you’re joining a community that’s here for you every step of the way. Let’s make this your best chapter yet!

Your tribe awaits!

What’s a roof over your head without a space where you can be part of something great. At CJ Students, we know that your student accommodation is more than a student room – it’s where friendships are formed, ideas are shared, and life-long memories are made. That’s why we’ve created a vibrant, inclusive community that gives you a sense of belonging, collaboration, and growth. Whether it’s through our social events, support programmes, or recreational activities, we’ll make it an experience you want to share.

 

Our community is built on four key pillars: Wellness, Mental Health, Sports & Recreation, and Social Engagement. These pillars are the foundation of a space where everyone feels valued and supported. Join fitness classes, enjoy group meditation, or take part in a friendly football match – there’s always something to bring us together. For those focused on academic success, we offer study groups, workshops, and mentoring programmes designed to help you excel. Plus, our seasonal celebrations and cultural events add an extra layer of fun and connection.

 

More than a residence, CJ Students is where you’ll find a home away from home. Feel supported by your peers, create cherished memories, and enjoy an environment that encourages you to achieve your best. Because here, it’s not just about living – it’s about thriving.
