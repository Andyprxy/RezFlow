Ask us a question ↗
What is RezFlow?

RezFlow is a connected accommodation operations platform. It brings applications, bed inventory, maintenance, inspections, communications, and management reporting into a shared workflow for residents and property teams.

Is RezFlow a student accommodation booking website?

RezFlow provides the system that helps residences manage their operations and resident journeys. The demo environments on this page are not accommodation listings or confirmed vacancies. For a room, contact the relevant residence directly.

Who is RezFlow designed for?

RezFlow is designed for private student accommodation, purpose-built student accommodation (PBSA), university residence operations, and multi-property residential portfolios. Each implementation is scoped around the operator’s needs.

Can I explore the Pretoria residence demos?

Yes. Choose Phumelela Residence, Adowa Living, or TKC Properties in the demo section and select a role. Some environments may require demo credentials. Contact admin@rezflow.co.za for access or a guided walkthrough, and use sample information only.

Do students need to download an app?

The platform blueprint uses a mobile-first web portal, so the student experience can be accessed through a browser. Messaging and other access routes depend on the configuration of the residence.

Can RezFlow support bursary-funded student workflows?

The platform scope includes funding document collection, verification queues, occupancy records, and reporting exports. Requirements and availability are confirmed during implementation. This does not imply NSFAS accreditation, institutional endorsement, or guaranteed funding approval.

Can it connect with WhatsApp, billing, or access systems?

Messaging and billing integrations can be scoped to your package and providers. Smart utility and biometric access integrations are roadmap capabilities in the supplied platform blueprint, so their availability must be confirmed rather than assumed.

How are pricing, onboarding, and data handling agreed?

A proposal is based on portfolio size, modules, integrations, migration, and support. During discovery, confirm implementation responsibilities, user permissions, data retention, privacy terms, and the support scope before deploying live resident information.
